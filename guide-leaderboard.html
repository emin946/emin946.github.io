Leaderboard guide

How the global ranking works and how to climb it.

How the ranking works

Tank Battle: Cockpit War 3D has a global leaderboard showing the top 50 players. Your position is based on your progress through the missions. Each entry shows the player's name and the flag of the nationality they chose, so you can see which countries are leading.

Your result is sent automatically after you play – there is nothing extra to set up. The list refreshes regularly, so a new personal best may take a short moment to appear.

Tips for climbing

  • Consistency beats speed. Failing and replaying a mission costs time. Upgrade before attempting a level that keeps beating you.
  • Revisit the Hangar often. Small, regular upgrades keep your tank in step with the difficulty curve.
  • Pick a name you are proud of. Names are visible to everyone; please keep them friendly. Offensive names may be removed.

Fair play

The leaderboard is meant to be fun for everyone. Results that are clearly impossible may be removed.
