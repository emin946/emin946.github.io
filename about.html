About Snowdrop Studio

Snowdrop Studio is an independent, one-person game studio. Everything from programming and design to testing and publishing is done in-house, which means feedback from players goes straight to the person who builds the game.

Our games

Tank Battle: Cockpit War 3D is our current release on Google Play. It is built with the Unity engine and designed to run well on a wide range of Android phones.

How we work

  • Short play sessions that fit into a busy day.
  • Fair progression for players who never pay.
  • Ads only at natural breaks, and rewarded videos that are always optional.
  • Regular updates based on what players tell us.
