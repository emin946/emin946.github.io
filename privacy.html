Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Snowdrop Studio ("we") handles information on this website and in our game Tank Battle: Cockpit War 3D.

This website

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information directly. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes.

This website may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Our game

  • Advertising: the game uses Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the device advertising ID and related data to show and measure ads. Where required, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • Leaderboard: the player name and nationality you choose, together with your game progress, are stored using Google Firebase so they can be shown on the global leaderboard. Sign-in is anonymous; we do not ask for your real name or email address.
  • Settings and progress are stored on your device.

Children

Our game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings. To ask for your leaderboard entry to be deleted, contact us at esat.yilmazsubasi@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
